# Privacy Policy

ShrinkWatch EU does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- The extension runs only on the supported shop domains listed in `manifest.json`, and only on pages you open yourself.
- It reads the product name, price and pack size shown on that page to compute a price per kg, litre or piece.
- To detect shrinkflation it stores, in your browser's local extension storage, a short history per product: shop, product identifier (SKU/EAN or URL), pack size, price, currency and the date seen. It does not store your name, account, cart, orders, location or browsing history outside supported product pages.
- On Mercadona, whose pages hide prices until a postcode is chosen, the extension asks Mercadona's own site for the product data of the page you opened. This request goes only to tienda.mercadona.es, like the shop page itself.
- No data is sent to any other server. There are no analytics, trackers, cookies or remote code.
- You can delete everything at any time with "Clear stored history" in the extension popup, or by removing the extension.
