Frequently asked questions

Supported shops, privacy, permissions and the shrinkflation warning.

Install free in 1 minute

Which shops does it work on?

It supports 71 grocery and drugstore web shops, 66 of them verified on real product pages. In the big markets that includes Lidl, Kaufland, Rewe, dm, Rossmann and Netto in Germany, Carrefour, Monoprix and Auchan in France, Albert Heijn in the Netherlands, Mercadona, Dia, Alcampo and Eroski in Spain, Conad in Italy, Auchan and Carrefour in Poland, and ICA, Willys, Mathem and Nemlig in Sweden and Denmark. See the shop guides and the full list.

Does it collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no network request. The prices and pack sizes you have seen are stored only in your browser, and removing the extension deletes them. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does it ask for access to each shop separately?

Only a few shops are switched on at install. For the others you click "Enable on this shop" in the popup, and the browser asks for access to that one shop. That way the extension never reads sites you did not choose.

How does the shrinkflation warning work?

Each time you open a product page, the extension saves the pack size and price locally. If you later open the same product and the pack is smaller for the same or a higher price, it shows a warning such as "was 500 g, now 450 g, +11.1% per kg". It only knows about products you have looked at before.

The shop already shows a unit price. Why do I need this?

EU shops must show a unit price under Directive 98/6/EC, but online it is often small, missing on some items, or simply wrong. The badge works out the figure from the price and pack size, cross-checks the shop's own number and tells you when they disagree. More on the rules.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, and Firefox 140 or newer. Install steps are on the home page.

The badge says "Pack size not found". What now?

Some shops print the pack size only on the product photo. Please open an issue with the product link, and it will be looked at.

My shop is not supported. Can it be added?

Yes. Open an issue with the shop name and a product link.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no affiliate links.
